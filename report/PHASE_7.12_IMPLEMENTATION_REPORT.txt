================================================================================

1. ZERO PARALLEL LOGGING SYSTEM:
   `PluginDiagnosticsEngine` does NOT implement a secondary event logging store. It acts strictly as a pure aggregator and correlator over already-generated structured upstream records:
   - Discovery findings & status (Phase 7.4 `DiscoveryResult`, `DiscoveredPluginEntry`)
   - Registration inventory (Phase 7.3 `PluginRegistry`, `RegisteredPlugin`)
   - Contract validation & configuration validation (Phase 7.1 & 7.5)
   - Dependency graph resolution & findings (Phase 7.6 `DependencyResolutionResult`)
   - Lifecycle state machine & transition records (Phase 7.7 `PluginLifecycleManager`, `PluginInstanceRecord`)
   - Security & permission audit logs (Phase 7.8 `PermissionAuditRecord`)
   - Execution runtime history, timeouts, and batch results (Phase 7.9 `ExecutionAuditRecord`, `PluginExecutionResult`)
   - Persistence snapshots & recovery action logs (Phase 7.11 `PersistedPluginState`, `PluginRecoveryActionRecord`)

2. REPORTING GRANULARITIES:
   - Per-Plugin Diagnostic (`PluginInstanceDiagnostic`): Complete history of an individual plugin instance from discovery through current state.
   - System-Wide Summary (`PluginSystemDiagnosticSummary`): Triages the entire ecosystem into healthy, failed, permission-violated, and orphaned plugins.

3. DUAL-FORMAT DETERMINISTIC RENDERING:
   - Markdown: Clean, readable diagnostic report organized by lifecycle stage.
   - JSON: Structured, machine-consumable representation.
   - Parity & Determinism: Both renderers are pure formatting functions over the same underlying data model with byte-identical output across repeated runs.
