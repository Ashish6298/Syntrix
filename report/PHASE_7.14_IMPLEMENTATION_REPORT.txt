  CLARIFICATION OF REUSE AND TRUST-LEVEL BOUNDARY CRITERIA
================================================================================
The reuse verification test (Test #2) previously summarized that "permission approval in real PluginPermissionGate directly transitions classification from BLOCKED to TRUSTED."
To ensure zero ambiguity, the test suite and classification logic have been explicitly broken out into separate boundary tests:
1. Scenario (a) [Test 2a]: The plugin had verified author provenance ('FPS Core Team') and zero other risk signals; the sole unapproved permission was the ONLY blocking condition. Once approved in `PluginPermissionGate`, every remaining criterion for `TRUSTED` was already satisfied, so transitioning to `TRUSTED` is mathematically correct.
2. Scenario (b) [Test 2b]: An unverified third-party plugin whose sole issue was an unapproved permission transitions from `BLOCKED` to `VALIDATED` (not `TRUSTED`) upon permission approval, confirming that reaching `TRUSTED` strictly requires verified provenance.
3. Provenance Override Protection [Test 2c]: A plugin with verified provenance ('FPS Core Team') that possesses an active heuristic risk signal (e.g. `process.execute` + `network.access`) is capped at `RESTRICTED` and cannot reach `TRUSTED`. Provenance alone never overrides active risk signals.
